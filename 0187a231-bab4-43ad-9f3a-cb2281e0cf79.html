Composition (Staging)

In this video, Dan walks us through staging an effect from a storyboard, with composition examples.

Effects are not always the star of a shot, so it is important to identify what kind of shot you are designing for! If you are designing a hero shot, where the effect is the focus, then you can go big and flashy with your design. If your effect is meant to be secondary, then it's important to make sure not to detract from the primary focus.
