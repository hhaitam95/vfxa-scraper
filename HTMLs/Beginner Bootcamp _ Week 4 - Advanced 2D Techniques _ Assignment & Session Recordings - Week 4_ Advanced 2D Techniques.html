Week 4: Advanced 2D Techniques

Week 4: Advanced 2D Techniques

Welcome back!

Previously, you have learned about the elemental principles and discovered the essential Photoshop tools.

This week is all improving on these concepts and honing your skills in either one of your chosen paths: Concept art or 2D animation.

And, after learning the theory, you will finish this week with your own concept art or 2D animation. Ready? ;)

 

 Weekly Lessons:

  • Color is the thing that keeps us away from a roaring fire, what makes us shiver when seeing an icy projectile, and what gives us comfort when in the middle of a healing spell. It can make or break so much of what we want to achieve with our effects. Learn how to wield this power in 2D and 3D by brushing up on the Color Foundations!
  • Elemental Foundations: Backed up by physics and real-life examples, Dan Elder teaches us various principles that will take your 2D effects to the next level!
  • Advance your concept painting skills with the FX Concept Painting Elemental Studies and draw one of four epic elemental explosions!

Assignment #4 - Advanced 2D techniques:

  • Create your own forum post in the Week 4 Forum on the VFX-A Discord
  • Watch the Color Foundation lecture!
  • Continue your chosen path from the previous week!
    • 2D Animation Path
      • Complete one of the assignments found at the end of each lesson in Lectures: Elemental Foundations
  • OR
    • Concept Art Path:
      • Choose and work on one of the 4 concept arts from FX Concept Painting Elemental Studies
