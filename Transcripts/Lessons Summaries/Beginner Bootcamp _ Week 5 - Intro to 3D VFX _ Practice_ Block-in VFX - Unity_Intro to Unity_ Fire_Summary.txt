TITLE: Intro to Unity: Fire
OVERVIEW: This lesson introduces Unity particle systems through a deliberately simple fire effect. It explains the basic particle-system setup, particle lifetime, size, color, emission, shape, and velocity, while encouraging a playful “what happens if” mindset. It is intended for complete beginners to Unity VFX.
KEY TOPICS:
- Unity Particle System basics
- Materials and transparent particle shaders
- Lifetime, size, color, and rotation
- Emission
- Spawn shapes
- Velocity over Lifetime
- Controlled randomness
TOOLS / TECHNIQUES / SHORTCUTS:
- Hierarchy > Effects > Particle System
- F to frame/center the selected effect
- URP > Particles > Unlit
- Surface Type > Transparent
- Random Between Two Constants
- Sphere spawn shape
- Particle color in pale gold
- Velocity over Lifetime
STEP-BY-STEP TAKEAWAYS:
1. Create a Particle System from the Hierarchy and zero its position/rotation while keeping scale at one.
2. Frame the effect, remove start speed so particles stay visually controlled, and create a transparent URP particle material.
3. Assign the material to the renderer and use it to establish the general size, shape, and color of the fire.
4. Randomize particle lifetime and size with small ranges, add broad rotation randomness, and use a pale-gold starting color.
5. Increase the emission rate, use a small sphere as the spawn region, and add Velocity over Lifetime rather than relying only on Start Speed.
6. Continue adjusting the modules down the inspector, using small random ranges to avoid uniform, mechanical particles.

ASSIGNMENTS / TIPS:
- Do not worry about mastering every particle-system setting at once.
- Use narrow random ranges to bridge uniform behavior and uncontrolled chaos.
- Experiment deliberately; the instructor presents multiple valid ways to build effects.
- Check the particle in the scene, not only in the inspector preview.
