TITLE: Waterfall Breakdown
OVERVIEW: This lesson rebuilds a waterfall as layered real-time elements, using a flowing main body, foam, droplets, and environmental interaction. It reinforces the earlier UV/vertex-color workflow while showing how multiple particle layers can add breakup and depth. It is intended for artists practicing natural environmental VFX in Unity.
KEY TOPICS:
- Waterfall body
- UV flow
- Foam
- Droplets
- Splash
- Vertex/UV control
- Layered particles
TOOLS / TECHNIQUES / SHORTCUTS:
- Waterfall mesh
- Panning texture
- Vertex colors
- Foam texture
- Particle droplets
- Collision/splash particles
- Material parameters
STEP-BY-STEP TAKEAWAYS:
1. Establish the large waterfall body with a simple flowing mesh and panning material.
2. Use UV layout and/or vertex colors to vary the flow and divide calmer water from foam.
3. Add foam layers around the edges and impact area.
4. Add droplets and small splash particles for breakup and secondary motion.
5. Ground the waterfall with a collision/impact region so the lower section feels connected to the world.
6. Tune the density and motion from the gameplay camera.
KEY MOMENTS:
- [00:00:00] The waterfall reference is introduced.
- [00:05:00] The main body and flow are established.
- [00:10:00] Foam is added.
- [00:15:00] Droplets and splash particles are introduced.
- [00:20:00] The impact/ground interaction is checked.
- [00:25:00] The complete layered waterfall is reviewed.
ASSIGNMENTS / TIPS:
- Solve the main flow before adding droplets.
- Use foam selectively.
- Keep secondary particles smaller and less dominant than the waterfall body.
