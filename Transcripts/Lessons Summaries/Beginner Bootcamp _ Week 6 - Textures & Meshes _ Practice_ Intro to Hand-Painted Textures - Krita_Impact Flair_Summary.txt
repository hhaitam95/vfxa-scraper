TITLE: Impact Flair
OVERVIEW: This lesson paints a reusable impact flare/sparkle texture from a soft central glow and varied rays. It emphasizes randomness, visual variety, layered sharp and soft shapes, and using a texture's intended story and game style to guide the design. It is aimed at artists building flexible hand-painted impact textures for VFX.
KEY TOPICS:
- Impact flare and sparkle design
- Central glow
- Ray construction
- Randomness and variety
- Sharp versus soft layers
- Texture reuse
- Style and story
TOOLS / TECHNIQUES / SHORTCUTS:
- 512 x 512 texture
- Airbrush
- X to swap colors
- V for Line tool
- E for eraser
- Brush pressure for tapering rays
- Gaussian Blur
- Basic 5 Size brush
- Grid for center alignment
- Brush spacing adjustment
STEP-BY-STEP TAKEAWAYS:
1. Decide what kind of impact the texture should communicate and create a 512 x 512 black-background texture.
2. Paint a soft white central glow on one layer.
3. Add another layer of rays using the Line tool, starting with light pressure and increasing pressure toward the center so each ray fades into the glow.
4. Vary ray length, placement, and thickness to avoid a symmetrical stick-like result, then erase or add rays to improve the composition.
5. Add a harder-edged brush layer that follows the general ray directions, again using pressure to create tapered sharp rays.
6. Blur the soft layer and retain enough crisp structure in the sharp layer so the final texture reads as both bright and graphic.
7. Check the result in the context of the intended effect and use additional variation rather than duplicating identical arms.

ASSIGNMENTS / TIPS:
- Think about the source and purpose of the impact before choosing its shape.
- Randomness and variety are important, but should remain compositionally intentional.
- Avoid perfectly symmetrical rays unless the style specifically calls for it.
- Keep a combination of soft glow and sharper structure for a versatile flare.
- Use the grid if centering the rays is difficult, but the instructor generally eyeballs placement.
