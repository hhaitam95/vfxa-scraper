TITLE: Goo Splat - Thumbnails
OVERVIEW: This lesson demonstrates creating several quick black-and-white thumbnail options for a toxic, viscous goo explosion. It explores splash, cylindrical, bubble, and spiky constructions while considering surface tension, area of effect, asymmetry, and directional motion. It is intended for artists practicing rapid ideation before committing to a detailed concept.
KEY TOPICS:
- Rapid thumbnailing
- Viscous goo shape language
- Circular/cylindrical AOE forms
- Surface tension
- Tendrils
- Asymmetry
- Negative space
- Reference
TOOLS / TECHNIQUES / SHORTCUTS:
- F for the recorded interface mode
- Horizontal canvas flip
- Black-and-white thumbnails
- Layer naming
- Sketchbook/reference use
- Lasso-style rough shape blocking
STEP-BY-STEP TAKEAWAYS:
1. Start with the green toxic-goo prompt and create several small black-and-white directions.
2. Explore splash-like/cylindrical shapes, sticky tendrils, and bubble-led internal explosions.
3. Keep the action contained to the intended AOE and use negative space to organize the composition.
4. Make the forms increasingly asymmetrical and directional, radiating outward from the center.
5. Flip the canvas to inspect imbalances and add/remove connecting pieces as needed.
6. Select a promising idea and move it into the full painting while keeping the thumbnail as a flexible direction rather than a rigid final silhouette.

ASSIGNMENTS / TIPS:
- Keep thumbnails quick and small.
- Use several options to explore direction instead of overworking one.
- Use real liquid reference to understand breakup and surface tension.
- Keep complex detail organized within a simple overall shape.
- Look for useful negative spaces and avoid accidental tangents.
