TITLE: Begin to Animate!
OVERVIEW: This lesson is the first part of a Toon Boom Harmony introduction for special effects artists and walks through making a complete first animation. It covers creating and saving a scene, setting up guides and basic drawing, animating a center-colliding effect with onion skin, carving negative space, and exporting the result. It is intended for beginners who want an end-to-end first exercise in Harmony.
KEY TOPICS:
- Harmony scene creation and saving
- Safe Area and drawing guides
- Frame-by-frame animation
- Onion Skin
- Negative-space carving
- Timing, holds, and dissipation
- Movie export
TOOLS / TECHNIQUES / SHORTCUTS:
- Ctrl+S to save
- Spacebar to pan the camera view
- 1 and 2 to zoom
- Alt+Shift for straight lines and rotation
- Alt+S for the Select tool
- Period/comma for next/previous frame
- Alt+O for Onion Skin
- Delete to remove selected artwork
- U to undraw selected areas
- Alt+I for Paint Bucket
- File > Export > Movie
- H.264 MPEG4 export
STEP-BY-STEP TAKEAWAYS:
1. Create and save a Harmony scene, set the brush size, and use Safe Area and simple guides to locate the center of the frame.
2. Draw several dots around the center guide and animate them clockwise toward the middle, using onion skin to maintain the progression.
3. Create the impact ball, animate it growing with progressively smaller size changes, and use negative-space carving to dissolve it afterward.
4. Offset the timing of different carved areas, add small leftover motion, and adjust the playback range so the sequence can be reviewed cleanly.
5. Restore the intended color and export the selected animation range as an H.264/MPEG4 movie.

ASSIGNMENTS / TIPS:
- Save the scene early.
- Use guides for planning, then remove or disable guide-only artwork from the final result.
- Vary the timing of negative-space elements rather than starting all of them together.
- Review the entire timeline and adjust the playback range before exporting.
- The lesson notes that QuickTime is needed for the demonstrated video-export workflow.
