TITLE: Design Commentary
OVERVIEW: This lesson presents a compact checklist of visual design principles that can be applied across effects. It develops big/medium/small variation into negative-space control, variation within a design theme, asymmetry, thick and thin, line weight, and depth. It is aimed at beginners who want a practical set of design considerations to apply deliberately while drawing and animating FX.
KEY TOPICS:
- Big, medium, and small
- Negative space
- Shape variation within a theme
- Asymmetry
- Thick and thin
- Line weight
- Depth and overlap
- Intentionality
TOOLS / TECHNIQUES / SHORTCUTS:
- Positive/negative-space planning
- Shape variation
- Asymmetric staging
- Thick/thin contour planning
- Layer and depth separation
- Overlapping lines
- Drawing through during rough animation
STEP-BY-STEP TAKEAWAYS:
1. Establish a clear large, medium, and small hierarchy instead of repeating equally sized forms.
2. Push negative-space shapes into positive forms so silhouettes become more interesting and dissipations have room to develop.
3. Choose a shape language or theme and vary the individual forms inside that theme rather than copying the same contour repeatedly.
4. Favor one side, direction, or shape to create asymmetry that supports the energy of the effect.
5. Use thick and thin areas intentionally, including line-weight variation that separates planes and directs attention.
6. Add depth through overlap, background/foreground separation, and shapes that visibly pass around or behind the object causing the effect.
7. Apply the principles deliberately across simple exercises such as fire, water, smoke, magic, or other effects.

ASSIGNMENTS / TIPS:
- Practice with intention: notice where you are repeating shapes and deliberately introduce variation.
- Do not equate symmetry with correctness; favor directional energy where appropriate.
- Use negative space as an active design element.
- Maintain a consistent line-weight approach once a style is chosen.
- Add depth through overlap when the effect should feel embedded in the world.
- Apply the concepts across many simple effects and keep the process creative and enjoyable.
