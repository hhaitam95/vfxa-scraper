TITLE: Damage Impact - Timing & Punch
OVERVIEW: This lesson builds a compact Niagara hit impact by starting with the minimum gameplay read and then adding secondary motion and controlled falloff. The emphasis is on immediate responsiveness, punchy timing, white-hot impact color, narrow randomness, velocity alignment, and deliberate extra emitters when they improve the read. It is aimed at beginners learning to block in gameplay VFX in Niagara.
KEY TOPICS:
- Timing and punchiness
- Gameplay readability
- Secondary motion
- Radial particle motion
- Velocity alignment
- Damping and speed curves
- Controlled randomness
- Impact color progression

STEP-BY-STEP TAKEAWAYS:
1. Start with a single short-lived flare because the gameplay purpose is immediate confirmation that a hit connected.
2. Give the flare one burst particle, a very short randomized lifetime, large starting size, random rotation, and a fast growth curve with a small taper so it pops immediately without feeling static.
3. Duplicate the flare for spike particles and introduce narrow randomness in count, lifetime, size, and radial/point velocity.
4. Change the sprite renderer to Velocity Align so the triangular particles point in the direction of travel, then add drag/gravity or other forces to give the secondary motion physicality.
5. Shape the velocity and size curves so the particles decelerate and disappear cleanly instead of abruptly popping out.
6. Use Color over Life to start with a white-hot flash and quickly cool into the target effect color.
7. Add additional tiny emitters only when they materially improve intentionality and gameplay readability, then compare the block-in against references.

ASSIGNMENTS / TIPS:
- Start with the gameplay read before adding visual decoration.
- Use narrow random ranges to add organic variation without losing cohesion.
- Do not make a short impact unnecessarily complicated.
- Use quick taper/falloff curves so particles retain punch but do not simply pop on or off.
- Judge additional emitters by whether they improve the gameplay read and visual intentionality.
