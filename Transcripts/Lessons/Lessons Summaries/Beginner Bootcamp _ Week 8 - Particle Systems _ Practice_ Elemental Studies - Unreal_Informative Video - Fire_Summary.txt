TITLE: Informative Video - Fire
OVERVIEW: This lesson studies real fire footage to extract useful animation behaviors for Unreal VFX. It focuses on flame mass, upward motion, breakup, turbulence, intensity changes, and small-scale detail that can later become separate emitters or textures. It is intended for artists building fire reference knowledge.
KEY TOPICS:
- Real fire reference
- Flame mass
- Turbulence
- Breakup
- Intensity
- Large/medium/small detail
TOOLS / TECHNIQUES / SHORTCUTS:
- Slow-motion video
- Frame-by-frame study
- Silhouette analysis
- Timing observation
STEP-BY-STEP TAKEAWAYS:
1. Study several fire clips in slow motion.
2. Identify the primary flame mass and the direction of its motion.
3. Observe tongues, detachments, and small turbulent details.
4. Note changes in speed and intensity over time.
5. Translate these behaviors into separate reusable VFX layers.
KEY MOMENTS:
- [00:00:00] Real fire reference begins.
- [00:05:00] Large flame forms are discussed.
- [00:10:00] Turbulent flame tongues are examined.
- [00:15:00] Breakup and detached pieces are discussed.
- [00:20:00] Intensity changes are translated into VFX ideas.
ASSIGNMENTS / TIPS:
- Study fire frame by frame.
- Focus on behavior before texture detail.
- Build from large to small.
