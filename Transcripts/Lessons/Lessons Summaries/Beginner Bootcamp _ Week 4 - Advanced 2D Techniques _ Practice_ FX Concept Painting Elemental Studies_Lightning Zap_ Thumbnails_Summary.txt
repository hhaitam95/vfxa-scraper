TITLE: Lightning Zap - Thumbnails
OVERVIEW: This lesson explores several quick black-and-white directions for turning a lightning-inspired shape into an AOE explosion. It focuses on finding strong thick-and-thin shapes, supporting elements, impact-center placement, negative-space relationships, and dimensional ground effects. It is intended for rapid visual exploration before a full painted concept.
KEY TOPICS:
- Lightning thumbnailing
- Impact AOE
- Thick and thin
- Supporting secondary shapes
- Negative space
- Centered/radiating design
- Three-dimensional ground impact
TOOLS / TECHNIQUES / SHORTCUTS:
- Layer duplication and opacity
- Lasso and quick sketching
- Side-by-side thumbnail layout
- Reference doodles
- Layer Styles
- Canvas composition adjustments
STEP-BY-STEP TAKEAWAYS:
1. Hide the source reference enough to work from its shape language, then create several quick options rather than one final design.
2. Explore bubble/plasma, ground-impact, and spiky lightning directions while keeping the visual energy organized around an epicenter.
3. Use thick-and-thin changes plus smaller supporting marks to reinforce the main form instead of letting every line compete equally.
4. Consider whether the effect should push upward, downward, or outward from the impact center and use the ground plane to suggest dimension.
5. Combine promising ideas when the thumbnails reveal complementary strengths, then carry the strongest composition into painting.

ASSIGNMENTS / TIPS:
- Keep thumbnail lines quick and exploratory.
- Use the source shape language and reference as inspiration instead of reinventing every form.
- Make secondary shapes reinforce the major motion.
- Use a clear epicenter for radial effects.
- Try different color directions later rather than locking into one palette during thumbnails.
