TITLE: Sketch - Drawing with Confidence
OVERVIEW: This lesson is a short practical sketch exercise using electricity to apply confident-stroke principles in a finished FX design. The instructor begins with large line-of-action strokes, develops the surrounding shapes with lighter marks, and then strengthens the chosen final lines with more pressure. It is aimed at practicing a fast transition from loose idea to readable stylized sketch.
KEY TOPICS:
- Line of action
- Light construction strokes
- Final weighted lines
- Electricity design
- Sparks and secondary details
- Filling negative space
TOOLS / TECHNIQUES / SHORTCUTS:
- Fast, light construction strokes
- Slow, pressured final lines
- Layer separation as a digital equivalent to paper line-weight differences
- Adding small sparks to expand a motion region
STEP-BY-STEP TAKEAWAYS:
1. Establish the main line of action with a few large strokes.
2. Build the overall silhouette around that line using quick and light marks.
3. Add secondary branches, sparks, and internal shapes to develop the electrical effect.
4. Decide which parts need to be strongest, then redraw the final contours with slower, heavier strokes.
5. Add or remove small details to balance empty space and finish the composition.

ASSIGNMENTS / TIPS:
- Start broad and light before committing to final lines.
- Use different line types and weights to make sketching easier.
- Add secondary sparks when they help fill space or expand the visual action.
- Do not overwork the sketch once the main idea reads clearly.
