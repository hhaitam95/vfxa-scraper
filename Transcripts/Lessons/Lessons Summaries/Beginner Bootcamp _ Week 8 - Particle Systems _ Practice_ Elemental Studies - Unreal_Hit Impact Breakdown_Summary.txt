TITLE: Hit Impact Breakdown
OVERVIEW: This lesson reconstructs a compact hit impact in Unreal by separating the flash, radial burst, streaks, and secondary follow-through. It emphasizes short lifetimes, velocity alignment, color timing, and narrow random ranges so the effect stays punchy. It is intended for artists creating gameplay-responsive impacts in Niagara.
KEY TOPICS:
- Impact flash
- Radial burst
- Directional streaks
- Color/lifetime curves
- Velocity alignment
- Secondary particles
TOOLS / TECHNIQUES / SHORTCUTS:
- Spawn Burst Instantaneous
- Initialize Particle
- Scale Sprite Size
- Shape Location
- Point Velocity
- Velocity Align
- Drag
- Gravity
- Scale Color
STEP-BY-STEP TAKEAWAYS:
1. Build the flash as the shortest and strongest layer.
2. Add radial particles with narrow random lifetime, size, and velocity ranges.
3. Align directional particles to their movement.
4. Use color and size curves for a rapid white-hot impact and quick cooling.
5. Add small secondary particles with slightly longer follow-through.
6. Review the entire hit at gameplay speed and simplify anything that weakens the punch.
KEY MOMENTS:
- [00:00:00] The impact is broken into its main layers.
- [00:05:00] The flash layer is created.
- [00:10:00] Radial burst particles are added.
- [00:15:00] Directional particles are aligned to movement.
- [00:20:00] Color and lifetime are refined.
- [00:25:00] Secondary follow-through is reviewed.
ASSIGNMENTS / TIPS:
- Protect the first impact moment.
- Use controlled randomness.
- Keep the secondary layers subordinate.
