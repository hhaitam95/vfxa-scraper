TITLE: Step, Smoothstep & Dissolve
OVERVIEW: This lesson introduces Step and Smoothstep as threshold functions for turning continuous grayscale data into hard or soft masks. It then connects those masks to dissolve-style effects, introduces Fraction and Sine as reusable shader signals, and shows how values can be remapped into a 0–1 range. It is intended for beginners learning common mask-building operations.

STEP-BY-STEP TAKEAWAYS:
1. Feed a grayscale texture into Step and set a threshold that turns lower values into 0 and higher values into 1.
2. Replace Step with Smoothstep when a feathered transition is needed.
3. Adjust the lower and upper Smoothstep edges to isolate a particular range of the texture.
4. Use the resulting mask to drive a dissolve or other cutout effect.
5. Use Fraction when only the repeating fractional component of a value is needed and remap a Sine output into 0–1 when building cyclic signals.
6. Expose threshold values so the transition can be art-directed.
