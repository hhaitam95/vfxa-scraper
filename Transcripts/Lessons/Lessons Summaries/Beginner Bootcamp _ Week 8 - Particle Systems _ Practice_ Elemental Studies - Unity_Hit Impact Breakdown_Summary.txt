TITLE: Hit Impact Breakdown
OVERVIEW: This lesson rebuilds a stylized hit impact from its major gameplay beats: flash, burst, directional pieces, and lingering secondary particles. It focuses on very short lifetimes, rapid color changes, velocity-aligned elements, and controlled randomness. It is intended for artists practicing punchy gameplay feedback in Unity.
KEY TOPICS:
- Hit-impact structure
- Flash timing
- Radial burst
- Directional streaks
- Color progression
- Secondary particles
- Random variation
TOOLS / TECHNIQUES / SHORTCUTS:
- Burst emission
- Texture Sheet Animation
- Velocity alignment
- Stretched Billboard
- Color over Lifetime
- Size over Lifetime
- Limit Velocity/Damping
STEP-BY-STEP TAKEAWAYS:
1. Establish the central flash as the shortest and strongest layer.
2. Add a radial burst of small particles with narrow random ranges.
3. Add directional streaks or spikes using velocity alignment.
4. Use color and size curves so the effect is white-hot at the hit and quickly cools.
5. Add a small secondary layer for debris or sparks and give it slightly longer follow-through.
6. Review the entire impact at real game speed and remove details that weaken the initial punch.

- [00:00:00] The impact reference is introduced.

ASSIGNMENTS / TIPS:
- Keep the first frame strong.
- Use narrow randomness.
- Make the impact readable before adding secondary particles.
