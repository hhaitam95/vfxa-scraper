TITLE: Waterfall Breakdown
OVERVIEW: This lesson breaks a waterfall into a main flowing body, foam, droplets, and impact/splash layers in Unreal. It combines mesh/material flow with particle breakup so the effect has a strong large-scale read and believable small-scale detail. It is intended for artists practicing layered environmental VFX.
KEY TOPICS:
- Waterfall body
- Flowing UVs
- Foam
- Droplets
- Splash impact
- Material and particle layering
TOOLS / TECHNIQUES / SHORTCUTS:
- Waterfall mesh
- Panning material
- Vertex-color masks
- Foam particles
- Droplet particles
- Collision/impact particles
STEP-BY-STEP TAKEAWAYS:
1. Establish the main waterfall body and its continuous downward flow.
2. Use UV/material motion to create the main surface movement.
3. Add foam and edge breakup where the water interacts with the environment.
4. Add droplets and small secondary particles for scale and variation.
5. Build the impact/splash region at the bottom.
6. Tune density and motion from the intended camera until the large flow remains dominant.
KEY MOMENTS:
- [00:00:00] The waterfall reference is introduced.
- [00:05:00] The large body is built.
- [00:10:00] Flowing material motion is configured.
- [00:15:00] Foam and edge layers are added.
- [00:20:00] Droplets and splash are introduced.
- [00:25:00] The complete waterfall is reviewed.
ASSIGNMENTS / TIPS:
- Solve the main flow before adding droplets.
- Use foam selectively.
- Keep small particles subordinate to the main waterfall.
