TITLE: Creation & Dissipation
OVERVIEW: This lesson introduces creation and dissipation as the basic ways effects enter and leave a shot. It contrasts quick burst-style creations with expansions and then develops simple and more complex dissipation using shrinking forms and negative space. It is aimed at beginners building a reusable vocabulary for bringing effects on screen and removing them naturally.
KEY TOPICS:
- Effect creation
- Burst versus expansion
- Timing charts
- Dissipation through shrinking
- Ripple dissipation
- Positive and negative space
- Layered dissipation
TOOLS / TECHNIQUES / SHORTCUTS:
- Timing charts
- Burst-frame exploration
- Squash and stretch
- Negative-space carving
- Ellipse/circle ripple construction
- Frame-by-frame tracking
STEP-BY-STEP TAKEAWAYS:
1. Plan the creation with a timing chart and decide whether the effect should burst on quickly or expand more gradually.
2. Explore the first one or two frames as creative burst poses, using squash, stretch, or anticipation when appropriate.
3. For dissipation, choose where the effect should start disappearing based on the action rather than letting it remain on screen unnecessarily.
4. Start with the simplest shrinking or pinching method, then progress to negative space eating into the positive effect.
5. For ripples, let the leading edge slow while the dissipation catches up and progressively reduces the positive-space thickness until only a line or small pieces remain.
6. Add variation with irregular shapes, overlaps, and droplets only after the basic dissipation works.

ASSIGNMENTS / TIPS:
- Practice the ripple assignment at a difficulty appropriate to your current level.
- Plan enough room for the effect to continue moving while it dissipates.
- Do not rely on a single formula; the instructor presents multiple valid approaches.
- Start simple and increase complexity only after the base principle is working.
- Expect perspective ripples to require careful spacing and potentially many frames.
