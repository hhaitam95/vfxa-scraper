TITLE: Heal Impacts - Arcs & Ease In/Out
OVERVIEW: This lesson builds a Niagara healing effect while applying physical arcs, overall story arcs, and ease-in/ease-out timing. It shows how gameplay beats can be organized into a rising action, impact, and falloff, then reinforced with curved particle motion and deliberately shaped curves. It is aimed at artists learning to make gameplay VFX feel communicative and satisfying rather than merely decorative.
KEY TOPICS:
- Physical motion arcs
- Story arcs
- Ease in and ease out
- Gameplay telegraphing
- Healing effect timing
- Particle movement
- Curve shaping
TOOLS / TECHNIQUES / SHORTCUTS:
- Niagara Curves
- Point/arc-based particle motion
- Ease-in/ease-out S-curves
- Lifetime-driven animation
- Curve key editing
- Gameplay timing planning
STEP-BY-STEP TAKEAWAYS:
1. Identify the gameplay beats of the heal: wind-up/anticipation, the actual heal hit, and the falloff.
2. Plan the overall story arc so those beats communicate the gameplay event clearly.
3. Add physical arcs to the moving particles so their paths curve naturally rather than following arbitrary straight lines.
4. Shape timing curves with easing so energy builds into the impact and then settles out instead of moving at a constant rate.
5. Vary how much ease is used according to the gameplay need; effects requiring more warning can spend more time easing in.
6. Finish by checking the full effect as a communication system and deliberately adjust timing, arcs, and payoff rather than treating them as isolated settings.

ASSIGNMENTS / TIPS:

- Treat the overall effect as a story arc with a readable payoff.
- Vary ease lengths according to the gameplay requirement.
- Use extra ease-in time when an effect needs a stronger telegraph.
